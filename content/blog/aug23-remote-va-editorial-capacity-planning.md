Reserve capacity for exceptions. Broken access, a late source change, or a substantial review can consume the time planned for ordinary production. A small buffer makes the schedule honest. Review the counts weekly and adjust the workflow before judging individual speed. If drafts regularly wait for decisions, the useful intervention is more predictable owner coverage or narrower briefs, not pressure on the assistant to generate more unfinished pages.
